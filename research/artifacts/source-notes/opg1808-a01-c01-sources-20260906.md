## Classical Gallai locator

Anton Trygub, "Full Characterization of Color Degree Sequences in Complete
Graphs Without Tricolored Triangles", arXiv:2304.14579v1 (28 April 2023),
Theorem 1.1, attributed there to Gallai (1967):
https://arxiv.org/html/2304.14579v1

The theorem assumes the absence of rainbow triangles in an undirected coloured
complete graph. It provides at least two parts, one colour between each pair,
and at most two colours across all pairs. It makes no orientation assertion.
The source page identifies its licence as CC BY 4.0. No verbatim theorem
extract or full paper is stored here.

Relation to the admitted directed target: analogy only. The directed target
allows rainbow transitive triples and additionally requires uniform direction
between parts. Neither of those differences is discharged by the classical
theorem. C01's transitive rainbow triangle violates the classical hypothesis
and satisfies the directed hypothesis, so the theorem cannot certify C01.
